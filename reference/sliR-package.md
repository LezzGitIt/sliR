# sliR: Standardized Length Index and Allometric Data Simulation

Computes the Standardized Length Index (SLI) for size-correcting
appendage lengths, following the scaled mass index approach of Peig and
Green (2009)
[doi:10.1111/j.1600-0706.2009.17643.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1600-0706.2009.17643.x)
, and simulates allometric morphological data with controllable scaling
exponents, correlation structure, and measurement or transient error.
